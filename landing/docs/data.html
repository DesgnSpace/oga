Docs / Your data

Everything Oga keeps stays on your Mac

Oga runs entirely on your machine. The only thing that ever leaves it is the work itself — the brief and the files you allowed — sent to the account you picked for it.

What Oga keeps, and where

Task records and task copies live under ~/.oga:

WhatWhere
The work you've handed off, your workers, and project memories~/.oga/oga.db
One copy of your repository for each piece of work given its own branch~/.oga/worktrees/
Other local working state~/.oga/

Where a setting comes from

Settings come from three places. The first one that has an answer wins:

  1. .oga.yaml in the project itself.
  2. ~/.oga.yaml, your own settings for every project.
  3. Oga's own defaults — the accounts it found and the rules it ships with.

Not having either file is normal. To see what a folder actually resolves to, and which file each setting came from:

oga config

Freeing disk space

oga cleanup shows what old work is holding and deletes nothing until you say so:

oga cleanup
oga cleanup --older-than 30d --delete

Only finished, archived work is eligible. Anything still running, anything waiting on you, and project memories are never touched. Cleanup can remove a clean task copy, but the branch remains.

None of this follows you between machines. On a new Mac, sign back into each provider tool and add your accounts again in Oga's Settings.
